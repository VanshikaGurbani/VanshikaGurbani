I build ML systems at the intersection of research and real-world impact. Currently an AI Engineer & Researcher at the **Particulate Systems Lab at Rutgers**, working on physics-informed ML, pharmaceutical manufacturing simulation, and AI tooling for NSF grant workflows. I care about systems that are not just accurate, but actually useful.

## What I'm Building

- Physics-informed ML pipelines replacing MATLAB simulations for pharmaceutical granulation — R² = 0.9984 on 125 DEM simulation cases
- 3D population balance MPC engine using L-BFGS-B optimization for wet granulation process control
- AI tools for NSF proposal processing: KeyBERT clustering, HuggingFace summarization pipelines (BART, Pegasus, T5), and PuLP-based reviewer assignment
- [NutriPulse](https://github.com/VanshikaGurbani/NutriPulse) — production nutrition tracker with dual-API food search across 380K+ foods
